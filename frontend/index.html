About the System

About HydroQuote AI

Combining decades of hydropower engineering expertise with cutting-edge AI natural language understanding — a prompt-driven intelligent quotation platform for hydropower projects.

Prompt-Driven · Pure LLM · End-to-End

HydroQuote AI is built on IBM Watson Natural Language Understanding and watsonx.ai large language models. Turbine model selection, unit configuration, equipment cost calculation, commercial pricing, and Proforma Invoice generation are all encapsulated into a five-stage Prompt Pipeline.

Pricing rules, modification factors, and tax calculations are embedded directly into prompt templates, enabling fast iteration and consistent quotation generation. Rule updates take effect instantly with a single commit.

The system covers the full power range from 500kW to 5000kW and supports all major turbine types: Francis, Kaplan, Pelton, Bulb, Tubular, and Cross-flow — with bilingual customer input and commercial document output.
